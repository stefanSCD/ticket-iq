## Functional

- All approved requirements REQ-001 … REQ-030 (except drafts) verified with at least one passed execution on HiL or Vehicle.
- No open defects of severity Critical; open High defects only with an agreed workaround.

## Reconnection time KPI

Measured during the vehicle endurance campaign (single phone, reference phones RP-01, RP-03, RP-07, RP-09):

- **≥ 98 % of ignition cycles** reconnect within 10 s;
- no ignition cycle above 20 s;
- campaign size: at least 200 ignition cycles per reference phone.

## Diagnostics

- For 100 % of HiL executions of TEST-006, all expected events are present.

## Multi-phone

Two-phone scenarios are part of the endurance campaign; the number of cycles for this part is still to be defined (see PC-071).

## Sign-off

Sign-off by Product Owner, Test Lead and Release Manager in the release readiness review.
