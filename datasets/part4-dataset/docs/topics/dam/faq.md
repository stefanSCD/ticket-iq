## Context

An incident is an evidence record, not a finding of borrower liability. Technicians document what they observed and managers classify the incident before repair or financial questions are handled outside the v1.1 product.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-DAM-FAQ-01

Managers have 2 business days to triage a new damage or missing-accessory incident.

### TN-DAM-FAQ-02

A loan can show an incident without establishing that the borrower caused the damage.

### TN-DAM-FAQ-03

Closing a loan does not by itself authorize release of a safety-related equipment block.
