# Waiting lists and offers — Staff FAQ

Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

The waiting list offers a newly freed interval to an eligible borrower. An offer is a temporary hold rather than an automatic reservation; the borrower still has to accept before its deadline.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-WAI-FAQ-01

A waiting-list offer remains available for 30 minutes after creation.

### TN-WAI-FAQ-02

Failure to read an offer notification does not automatically accept the reservation.

### TN-WAI-FAQ-03

A training-ineligible entry is skipped for that offer without being silently deleted from the waiting list.
