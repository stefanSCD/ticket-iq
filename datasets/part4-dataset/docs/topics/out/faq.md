# Pickup and checkout — Staff FAQ

Document ID: `TN-OUT-FAQ`  
Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

Physical handover creates a loan from a confirmed reservation. Staff scan a unit label and verify the borrower rather than treating possession of a reservation number as sufficient identity proof.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-OUT-FAQ-01

Equipment may be collected from 30 minutes before the reservation starts.

### TN-OUT-FAQ-02

Scanning a different unit does not replace the reservation assignment; ask staff to use the explicit substitution workflow.

### TN-OUT-FAQ-03

If the confirmation response is lost, retry the same checkout request with the same Idempotency-Key.
