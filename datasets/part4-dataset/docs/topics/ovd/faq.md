# Overdue loans and account restrictions — Staff FAQ

Document ID: `TN-OVD-FAQ`  
Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

ToolNest v1.1 tracks late loans and borrowing restrictions without collecting money. Overdue state follows the physical receipt time; a delayed staff inspection must not make the borrower appear to keep equipment longer.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-OVD-FAQ-01

The first overdue email is sent 4 elapsed hours after the due time if the unit has not been received.

### TN-OVD-FAQ-02

A pending return inspection does not keep an overdue restriction active after receipt.

### TN-OVD-FAQ-03

Settling all overdue loans does not lift an unrelated manual suspension.
