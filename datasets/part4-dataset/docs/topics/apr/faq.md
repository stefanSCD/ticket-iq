# Reservation approval — Staff FAQ

Document ID: `TN-APR-FAQ`  
Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

High-value equipment uses a staff approval gate. An approval belongs to a specific request version; it is neither a standing privilege for a borrower nor permission to ignore an availability conflict.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-APR-FAQ-01

Undecided approval requests remain open for 48 hours before they expire.

### TN-APR-FAQ-02

Changing only a borrower-facing display note does not invalidate approval; changing the asset or interval does.

### TN-APR-FAQ-03

Managers cannot approve their own borrowing request; another manager for that lab must decide it.
