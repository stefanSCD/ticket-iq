# Equipment kits and bundled items — Staff FAQ

Document ID: `TN-KIT-FAQ`  
Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

Some borrowable equipment is assembled from several tracked units. A field-recording kit has one reservation and one borrower-facing loan but its component units still need individual availability and condition tracking.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-KIT-FAQ-01

A kit may include up to 16 required tracked units.

### TN-KIT-FAQ-02

A definition edit applies to future borrowing; an existing loan keeps its original component checklist.

### TN-KIT-FAQ-03

A failed kit booking must not leave temporary reservations on otherwise available components.
