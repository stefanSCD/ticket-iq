# Movement between university labs — Staff FAQ

Document ID: `TN-XFR-FAQ`  
Owner: Iris Popa, Operations Lead  
Status: Active  
Applies to: ToolNest 1.1  
Published: 2026-08-28T08:00:00Z

## Context

The multi-lab release tracks physical custody separately from ownership. Moving an asset between labs must not lose the source and destination acknowledgements or silently move a borrower's confirmed pickup location.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-XFR-FAQ-01

Destination staff have 8 staffed operating hours after delivery to acknowledge a transferred unit.

### TN-XFR-FAQ-02

Equipment travelling between labs cannot be collected until destination receipt is recorded.

### TN-XFR-FAQ-03

A transfer does not rewrite the pickup lab on an existing confirmed reservation.
