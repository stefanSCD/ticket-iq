## Context

Maintenance blocks protect equipment that is being inspected or repaired. Closing a work order is distinct from certifying a unit safe for use, and future reservations must be treated explicitly rather than disappearing from the system.

## Requirements and guidance

### TN-MNT-FAQ-01

Preventive maintenance is due every 120 calendar days after the last completed preventive service.

### TN-MNT-FAQ-02

An equipment repair does not automatically cancel an existing confirmed reservation.

### TN-MNT-FAQ-03

Completing a service job cannot reactivate a Retired asset.
